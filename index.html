1 second to 60 minutes. Changing either duration resets the current game timer and pauses it. Color-only changes keep the timer running.

1 second to 60 minutes between games.

Match sounds

Long whistle at kickoff · Voice announces “one minute remaining” · Three slower whistles at full time, with a long final blast. Spoken countdown from 5 to 1 before the next kickoff. Resume stays quiet. Check your device volume and keep the app visible.
