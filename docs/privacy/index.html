YOUR DATA, CLEARLY EXPLAINED

Privacy policy.

Touchline is designed to run on the sideline without accounts, purchases, advertising, or behavioral tracking. This policy explains what stays on your device and when our website host may process information.

EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 1, 2026

Overview

This privacy policy applies to the Touchline mobile app and the Aged Athletics website at agedathletics.com. Touchline is operated by Aged Athletics.

We do not require an account, sell in-app purchases, run advertising, or include analytics or behavioral-tracking software in Touchline.

Information in the app

Touchline stores the information needed to run your soccer sessions locally on your device. This may include:

  • team colors and the number of teams;
  • game, changeover, warning, audio, and session-ending settings;
  • the current rotation, timer state, and session statistics; and
  • display and keep-awake preferences.

Aged Athletics does not receive this locally stored session information. It remains on your device unless you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

Audio and other apps

Touchline plays bundled whistles, announcements, countdowns, and other match sounds. On supported devices, it requests temporary audio focus so music or other audio can become quieter during an announcement and return afterward.

Touchline does not use your microphone, record conversations, or collect the audio played by other apps.

Website visits

The Aged Athletics website does not use advertising cookies, analytics, account logins, or contact forms. It is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser type, device information, requested page, and timestamp to deliver and secure the site.

GitHub’s handling of that information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Links to Google Play, GitHub, and other external services are governed by those services’ own policies.

Sharing and retention

Aged Athletics does not sell or rent personal information. We do not share your locally stored Touchline session information with advertisers or data brokers.

App settings and session information remain on your device until you remove them by clearing app data or uninstalling Touchline. Technical website information is handled by GitHub according to GitHub’s policies.

Children

Touchline may be used by adults to organize youth sports, but the app and website are intended for coaches, organizers, and other people managing games. Touchline does not ask children—or any users—to provide names, contact details, precise locations, or account information to Aged Athletics.

Your choices

You control the settings and session information stored by Touchline. You can change them inside the app, clear the app’s storage through your device settings, or uninstall the app.

Changes and contact

We may revise this policy when Touchline’s features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revision is published.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Aged Athletics through the Touchline project’s public issue tracker.
